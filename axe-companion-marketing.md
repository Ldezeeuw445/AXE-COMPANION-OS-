## 2. Feature Section

- Cross-references four data layers in real time. Price action, news sentiment, account exposure, and behavioral patterns — combined into one read, not four separate tabs.
- Filters noise, not just data. AXE doesn't surface everything. It surfaces what changed market structure and how it relates to your current positions.
- Knows your risk state before you ask. It monitors position sizing, drawdown pace, and delta against expected ranges — and flags anomalies without you running the numbers.
- Behavioral awareness built in. Recognizes patterns across your own session history — overtrading windows, size drift, revenge timing — and flags them without judgment.
- Always on. Never reactive. AXE works in the background while you watch the tape. By the time you ask, it's already read the setup.
