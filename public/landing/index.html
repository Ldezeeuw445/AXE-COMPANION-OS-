FAQ

Honest answers.

Are the intel feeds real or illustration?
Real. All 19 AXE feeds — Intel Tide, Dark Pool, Options Flow, Insider Flow, Policy Flow, Global Chokepoints, Military Radar, Emergency Monitor, Vessel Intel, Jet Tracker, Energy Flow, Seismic Events, Cyber Intel, Correlation Engine, Déjà Vu, Macro, Market Conviction, News and Calendar — stream live.
Is this a real chart with live data?
Yes — Lightweight Charts wired to MT5 ticks, with auto-Fib, auto-trend, OB / FVG / iFVG, PDH/PDL, swings, BOS/CHoCH and a market-depth drawer over the live spread.
Can I really execute trades from AXE?
Both demo and live. New accounts get a free virtual demo on live prices. Live execution requires the master-password account and a one-time disclaimer banner.
What is the Cockpit FIT score?
It measures how aligned your real execution is with your trading plan, built from your MT5 fills — rules kept vs passed, a behavior map, and a four-week correction rhythm.
Do I need Trading OS on desktop?
No — chart, depth, intel, journal, accounts and AXE all work standalone. Trading OS is the upcoming desktop terminal sharing the same Supabase login.
Is this an App Store app?
Install the site as a PWA on your home screen — no App Store required for the core flow.
