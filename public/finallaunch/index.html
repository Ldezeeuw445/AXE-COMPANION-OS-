FAQ

Honest answers.

Are the intel feeds real or illustration?

Real. All 19 AXE feeds — Intel Tide, Dark Pool, Options Flow, Insider Flow, Policy Flow, Global Chokepoints, Military Radar, Emergency Monitor, Vessel Intel, Jet Tracker, Energy Flow, Seismic Events, Cyber Intel, Correlation Engine, Déjà Vu, Macro, Market Conviction, News and Calendar — stream live.

Is this a real chart with live data?

Yes. The chart layer is built on live MT5 account data, real ticks and the same execution-aware workflow shown in the app.

Can I really execute trades from AXE?

Yes. Demo spins up instantly on live ticks, and live execution is available once MT5 is connected and the account is explicitly armed for it.

What is the Cockpit FIT score?

It is AXE’s behavior alignment score. It tracks whether your real fills and session behavior are staying close to your rules or drifting away from them.

Do I need Trading OS on desktop?

No. AXE Companion is designed as a full phone-first product. Desktop becomes an additional surface, not a dependency.

Is this an App Store app?

Not yet. Right now the launch flow is web/PWA first so the product can move faster while keeping the same UX and execution model.
