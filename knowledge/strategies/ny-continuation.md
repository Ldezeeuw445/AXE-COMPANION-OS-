# Strategy card: NY continuation (template)

## Conditions
- London sets a **directional leg**; NY open **continues** rather than reversing (no immediate CHoCH against London impulse).
- Pullback into **discount** (long) or **premium** (short) of the London leg.
- Confluence: OB/FVG + resting liquidity not yet fully taken in trade direction.

## Invalidation
- Opposite **CHoCH** on trade timeframe before entry triggers.
- Calendar **high impact** surprise against position — flatten or reduce per playbook.
