# Strategy card: London sweep (template)

## Conditions
- Price **sweeps** PDH/PDL or Asian liquidity early London.
- **Displacement** in the reversal direction after the sweep (not only a wick).
- **FVG** or OB forms on the displacement leg; entry on retrace into PD array.
- Stop beyond the sweep extreme; targets toward **EQ** or opposing liquidity pool.

## Invalidation
- No displacement / immediate reclaim through sweep level with continuation in original direction.
- **Choppy** two-sided tape through London open without a clear sweep narrative.
- **Major news** within ~15 minutes without a pre-defined plan — stand down.

## Checklist
- [ ] HTF bias not fighting the reversal
- [ ] Session time inside London killzone window you trade
- [ ] Risk sized before entry
