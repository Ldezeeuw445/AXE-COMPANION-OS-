# Liquidity (AXE core)

## Pools
- **Buy-side liquidity (BSL)**: resting stops/orders above highs; price often attracts before distributing lower.
- **Sell-side liquidity (SSL)**: below lows; symmetric logic for shorts after a sweep.

## Sweeps
- A **liquidity sweep** takes resting levels then **rejects** — displacement back through the swept level adds confluence for reversal or continuation setups depending on HTF story.

## Equal highs/lows (EQH/EQL)
- Retail clusters; engineered stops. Sweeps of EQH/EQL are common **before** larger moves — treat them as fuel, not random noise.

## PD arrays
- Order blocks, FVGs, and breaker/mitigation concepts sit in **price delivery** logic: where did aggressive orders rest and where is the void to fill next?
