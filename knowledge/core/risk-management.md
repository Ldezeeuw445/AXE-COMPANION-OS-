# Risk management (AXE core)

## Position sizing
- Risk per trade is a **fixed fraction** of account or a fixed R-dollar amount — decided before entry, not after price moves against you.

## Daily / weekly guardrails
- Cap **max trades per day**, max daily loss, and **cooling-off** after consecutive losses. Revenge trades are a system failure, not a market problem.

## Correlation
- Multiple correlated positions (e.g. XAUUSD + DXY inverse stacks) can **double risk** without doubling edge — size as one macro bet.

## News
- Reduce size or **no new risk** into high-impact prints unless your plan explicitly allows event trading.
