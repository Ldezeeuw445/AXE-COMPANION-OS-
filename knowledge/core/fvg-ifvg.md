# FVG / iFVG (AXE core)

## Fair value gap (FVG)
- Three-candle imbalance: middle candle’s wick/body leaves a **gap** between candle 1 and 3 extremes. Represents unfilled aggressive flow; often acts as magnet on retracement.

## Inversion (iFVG)
- When price **fills** then **rejects** through an FVG, the gap can **invert** — former resistance becomes support (and vice versa). Invalidation is a clean close back through the inverted zone on the timeframe of the setup.

## Usage
- Prefer FVG confluence with **order flow displacement**, session killzone, and HTF discount/premium alignment. Avoid trading every FVG in chop.
