# Market structure (AXE core)

## Bias and breaks
- **BOS (break of structure)**: price closes beyond a swing in the trend direction; confirms continuation until invalidated.
- **CHoCH (change of character)**: first meaningful counter-trend break; often the earliest sign distribution/accumulation is shifting.
- **MSS / internal range**: lower timeframes build internal swings inside a higher-timeframe leg; entries often lean on internal BOS after external liquidity is taken.

## Premium / discount
- Inside a clear dealing range, the upper half is **premium** (better for sells), lower half **discount** (better for buys). Equilibrium is the mid — mean-reversion magnet unless a strong displacement trend proves otherwise.

## How AXE uses this
- Anchor **HTF bias** first, then map **session** and **LTF execution**. Do not flip bias on one counter candle without CHoCH context.
