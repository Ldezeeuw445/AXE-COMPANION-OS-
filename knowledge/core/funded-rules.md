# Funded account rules (AXE core)

## Common constraints (varies by firm)
- **Daily drawdown** and **max trailing drawdown** — know which binds first intraday.
- **Consistency rules**: cap on % of profit from a single day vs total payout window — avoid one lottery day masking poor process.

## Behaviour
- No **HFT / martingale / grid** unless explicitly allowed. Copy-trade restrictions may apply.

## Reporting
- Treat funded accounts as **risk budgets** with compliance strings — not “more leverage to YOLO”.

## AXE stance
- Never encourage violating firm rules. If rules unknown, remind trader to verify their contract and dashboard limits.
