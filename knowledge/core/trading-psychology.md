# Trading psychology (AXE core)

## Emotional cycles
- **FOMO** after a move = buying premium / selling discount late. **Revenge** after a loss = oversized next trade. Name the state; slow down.

## Process over outcome
- A good process with a loss is still repeatable; a bad win teaches the wrong lesson. Journal **decision quality**, not only PnL.

## Consistency
- Prop-style consistency often punishes **lumpy** days — smooth risk and avoid hero trades that save a bad week.

## Self-talk
- Replace “I was wrong” with “this **setup class** failed its invalidation rule” — keeps feedback actionable.
