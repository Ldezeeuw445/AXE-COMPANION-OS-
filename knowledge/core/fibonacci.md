# Fibonacci retracement (AXE core)

## Standard levels
- 0.382 / **0.5** / **0.618** are primary reaction zones in trending legs. 0.5 is equilibrium of the swing — frequent pivot for continuation entries.

## Application
- Anchor swing **clearly** (HTF leg or session range). Avoid overfitting every micro-swing on 1m.

## Confluence
- Fib **overlapping** OB or FVG increases quality; fib alone in news chop is low edge.

## Risk framing
- Stops belong beyond **structural invalidation** (swing / OB / sweep level), not arbitrarily at the next fib tick.
