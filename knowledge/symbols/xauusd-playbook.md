# XAUUSD playbook (AXE)

## Behaviour
- Often **volatile** around London open and NY overlaps; respects DXY and real-yield narratives but intraday is flow + liquidity engineering.

## Sessions
- **Asia** can compress range; **London** frequently sets the directional bias leg; **NY** continuation or reversal into PM.

## Liquidity
- Previous day / week highs and lows, equal highs/lows, and **round numbers** are magnets.

## Risk
- Wide spreads off-session; size down. Major USD prints can gap — reduce or flatten per your plan.
