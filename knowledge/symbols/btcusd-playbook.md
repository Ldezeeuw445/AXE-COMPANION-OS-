# BTCUSD playbook (AXE)

## Behaviour
- **24/7** tape; weekend gaps vs forex. Correlates risk-on/off but also idiosyncratic crypto catalysts (ETF flows, regulation headlines).

## Structure
- HTF ranges are wide — anchor swings on **4H/D** for bias; use LTF for execution only when HTF story is clear.

## Liquidity
- Equal highs/lows and obvious swing clusters still work; watch **funding** and crowded positioning as context, not a sole trigger.

## Risk
- Volatility spikes — stops can slip; size for gap risk.
