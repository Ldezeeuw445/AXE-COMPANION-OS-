# EURUSD playbook (AXE)

## Drivers
- ECB trajectory vs Fed, core CPI surprises, and broad **USD** impulse. Often cleaner trend legs than XAU on pure macro days.

## Sessions
- London liquidity builds the day’s range; NY continuation common. Asia range-break false starts happen — wait for London participation when in doubt.

## Levels
- **PDH/PDL**, Asian high/low, and prior week extremes are standard liquidity pools.
