# Indices playbook (ES/NQ/YM) (AXE)

## Character
- **Gap and go** vs gap-fill regimes; opening drive often sets tone for cash session. Correlation across indices — avoid accidental triple exposure.

## Levels
- Prior session OHLC, weekly pivots, and obvious **liquidity** above/below prior highs/lows.

## Risk
- Event risk (CPI/FOMC) can expand ranges — widen stops or cut size; know your futures margin and session maintenance.
