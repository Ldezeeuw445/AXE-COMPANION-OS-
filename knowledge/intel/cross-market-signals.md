# Cross-Market Signal Playbook

## Gold (XAUUSD)

- Energy stable + neutral tide → NEUTRAL unless Fed / real yields move
- Brent spike + chokepoint vessel alerts → bullish gold (inflation + geopolitical)
- Strong USD (DXY) headline week → caps gold even if tide bullish

## Bitcoin (BTCUSD)

- Risk-on tide + dark pool accumulation in BTC/COIN/MSTR → bullish continuation
- Cyber threats on exchanges + bearish options sweeps → caution / NEUTRAL
- Senate sells in crypto-adjacent names → watch regulatory headline risk

## Indices (NAS100 / US30)

- Market tide bearish + insider selling in mega-cap tech → risk-off
- Jets to DC + senate activity in defense → sector rotation, not always index direction

## FX (EURUSD)

- Energy shock hurts EUR energy importers; USD often benefits on flight to quality
- Geopolitical escalation in Europe → EUR risk premium

## Correlation examples (training)

1. Oil exec jets → Gulf + EIA draw + XAUUSD bid
2. Defense insider buys + ACLED escalation + call sweeps on LMT/NOC
3. Neutral tide + stable Brent + no chokepoint alerts → NEUTRAL XAUUSD (no catalyst)

Always name **feeds used** and **confidence** explicitly.
