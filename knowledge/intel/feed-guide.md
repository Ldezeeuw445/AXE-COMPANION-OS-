# Intel Feed Guide

symbol:XAUUSD symbol:BTCUSD symbol:EURUSD

## marketTide

Net call vs put premium across the tape. Neutral when call ≈ put (within ~5%). Bullish skew when call premium dominates; bearish when puts dominate. Pair with **unusualOptions** on the same symbol for confirmation.

## energyFlows

EIA-style series: Brent, WTI, nat gas, inventories. Stable energy → often **NEUTRAL** for gold unless geopolitical shock. Rising crude + Middle East conflict events → XAUUSD inflation / safe-haven read.

## corporateJets & vesselTracking

Executive travel to deal sites or conflict zones; tankers near **chokepoints** (Hormuz, Suez, Panama). Grounded fleet = quiet; airborne cluster toward one region = watch headlines 24–48h.

## darkPoolPrints & unusualOptions

Size matters. Prints >$10M notional or sweeps on weekly expiries often front-run catalysts. Cross-check **insiderTrades** same ticker within 5 sessions.

## conflictEvents / seismic

Tag source: ACLED (conflict), USGS (quake), EONET (natural). Map region to FX (USD), energy (USOIL), gold (XAUUSD), indices (risk-off → NAS100).

## cyberThreats

Financial-sector targeting + dark pool sell-side + bearish tide = risk-off cluster. Rare alone; strong as second feed.
