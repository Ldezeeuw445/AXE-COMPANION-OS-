# Trade review framework (AXE)

## After every trade
1. **Setup class** — what pattern was this (A+ / B / discretionary)?
2. **Plan adherence** — entry, size, stop, targets: followed or drifted?
3. **Market context** — session, news, volatility regime appropriate?
4. **Emotional state** — calm / rushed / revenge?
5. **One improvement** — single concrete tweak for next time.

## Scoring (optional)
- Rate **process** 1–5 independent of outcome. Track process score vs PnL over 30 trades.
